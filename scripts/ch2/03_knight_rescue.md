# CH2 Scene 3 — The Knight Rescue
**Slot:** `ch2_rescue` | **Length:** ~45s | **Beat:** 4 (CH2_PARIS.md) | **Canon:** Story Bible v0.4
**Music:** drum, rising.

**Setting:** the road outside the lists, next morning. A knight's horse has bolted; the knight is dragged by one stirrup toward the river bank.

1. KNIGHT (unnamed; cast list pending Leon): "Help! Anyone!"
2. Jack is just there, leading Tancrede.
3. Jack climbs on (up his neck; Tancrede sighs). Tancrede charges.
4. Jack holds out Bart's lance. The knight grabs it. Tancrede plants his hooves. The knight is pulled free a hand's width from the water.
5. The knight stands, muddy, and kneels.
6. KNIGHT: "You saved my life. My sword is yours." He holds it out.
7. Jack shakes his head. The helm spins.
8. KNIGHT: "Then my hand, in friendship!"
9. Jack shakes his head again.
10. KNIGHT (confused): "Then... what do you want?"
11. JACK: "..." He rides off. Tancrede knows the way.
12. KNIGHT (calling after): "I'll repay you, Sir... whoever you are!"

**Player input:** optional one-tap timing on the lance hold. Any timing succeeds; no fail state.
**Exit:** → `ch2_payoff` (filler and Bart's thread may play in between).

**Notes:** the knight is poor. Show patched armor and a rope bridle, which sets up the payoff.
