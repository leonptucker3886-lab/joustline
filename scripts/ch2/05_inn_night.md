3. Jack's room. He sets down the helm. He sits on the bed in the armor.
4. Quiet shots: the ledger with half crossed out. The too-big gauntlets. Tancrede visible through the window.
5. Jack draws Bart's sword and sets the hilt on the floor, point toward himself. He looks at it a long time. No expression.
   (Tone: kept dark and quiet. No blood, no fall shown. The scene is cut before anything happens.)
6. The door BANGS open.
7. CHARLES DE WIERD (thrilled): "He's alive! Lord Bart Sheen! He's heading for the Château de Sampsonne!"
8. Charles does not look at the sword. He has no idea.
9. CHARLES: "Isn't it wonderful? Goodnight!" He leaves. The door swings shut.
10. Jack looks at the door. Slowly slides the sword back into its sheath.
11. Jack gives the closed door a silent nod. (Jack only ever speaks to Bart.)
