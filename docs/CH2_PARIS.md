# Chapter 2 — "Paris" (outline, canon draft)

Chapter 1 is unchanged. Source: Leon Tucker, 2026-10-29 (12:31–12:42 UTC).

## Beat order
1. **Forced knighthood.** Jack is made a knight against his will. He rides in Bart's armor on Bart's horse, **Mildahouse**. The Baron buys Jack nothing. Jack is unhappy but silent. The Baron takes half of Jack's pay (clearly unfair).
2. **A few jousts.** Jack rides several jousts and tries nothing yet.
3. **The inn / the sword moment.** Jack is staying at the inn where Charles de Wierd checks in. Charles is preparing a rich envoy to go find Bart in the morning; Jack just happens to be there. Jack plans to fall on his sword. Charles bursts in, excited that he found Bart heading toward the castle, with no idea what Jack was planning.
4. **The knight rescue.** Jack saves a knight in immediate danger. The knight offers his sword and his hand; Jack turns them down.
5. **The payoff (later).** The Baron gives the struggling knight a horse and armor as payment for carrying the message that Bart has died. The knight tells Jack and rides off. OPEN: whether the knight knows the message is false.

## Where Bart really is
- Bart rolled off following a "butterfly" (slang for a pretty girl).
- He disappears 45 minutes before the plot to kill him — escapes by luck.
- He leisurely finds ale and merrymaking, telling patrons he may never walk again.
