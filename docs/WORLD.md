# Joustline: World and Flow
France from 1515 to 1559, following the travelling court. The map is drawn as an illuminated manuscript.

**Places:** Montrésor (home and estate), Amboise and Clos Lucé (Leonardo), Blois (court posts), Chambord (hunts), near Calais (Cloth of Gold), Pavia and Madrid, Fontainebleau, and Paris on the rue Saint-Antoine (1559).

## Flow on the phone
Sign in with LeonLink or play as a guest → map hub → event (2–4 story panels, then a joust, choice or mini-game) → results → estate → chapter finale.

**Seasons:** spring tournaments, summer war, autumn hunts, winter court.
**Daily 5-minute loop:** the quintain challenge, one tournament and collecting coin.

## Story locations (Story Bible v0.4, canon)
These three places are canon per Story Bible v0.4 (CANON_CHECK G8–G10). The knight is Lord Bart Sheen.

### Château de Sampsonne (G8)
- The endgame location, renamed from "Castle de Simpson" to avoid copyright (GEN call). Always write "Sampsonne".
- Bart reaches it after slipping away from the Baron and the Doctor in Paris by following a butterfly. There he learns he has become King because he was "just there", and he doesn't care.
- Bart keeps trying to give the crown to his cousin, the Baron of Vaudrecourt, who never hears him. The Baron gathers champions and pushes his weak claim, which leads to the epic battle.
- **Main ending:** the Final Joust against Bart at Sampsonne. Bart is healed and sober and still believes his debts are owed. Win, Lose and Yield all reunite knight and squire. Saving Henri II, Leonardo's helm and Catherine's favor feed into the battle.
- Also the stage for the alternate endings (Alt 1, Alt 2, Alt 3, Alt A, Alt B) and the Inspector joke ending ("zero fire exits").

### The burned inn on the Loire road (G9)
- About 6 years before 1515, Bart, lost and drunk on the Loire road, found a roughly 10-year-old boy at a burned inn, calmly holding a panicking horse (Tancrède). Bart took both and tells everyone he "won the boy at dice".
- Fragments only, never a full flashback: a scorched inn sign hangs in the Montrésor stable and nobody may take it down. In a Loire-road side quest the squire stops at the ruined gate and the scene holds, with no text.

### Paris (G10)
- An off-screen location. The player never sees Paris directly.
- Bart goes to Paris for surgery after the Trick. News reaches the squire only in fragments: a sober letter, an inn rumour, and the surgeon's bill in the ledger ("Surgeons, Paris: paid. Squire: received.").
- The squire sets out to reach Paris but is stopped by the Baron's paid knight, who claims Bart is dead. Charles de Wierd accidentally spills the truth, and the squire pursues Bart to Château de Sampsonne.
- Separate from the 1559 rue Saint-Antoine tournament in Paris on the historical map.
