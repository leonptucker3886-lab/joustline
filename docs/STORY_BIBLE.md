## 3. Backstory (shown only in fragments)
~6 years before 1515, Bart, lost and drunk on the Loire road, finds a ~10-year-old boy at a burned inn calmly holding a panicking horse (Tancrede). Bart takes both. He tells everyone he "won the boy at dice" — a lie to hide that he was kind.
Fragments: scorched inn sign in the stable nobody may take down; Loire-road side quest where the squire stops at the ruined gate and the scene holds, no text.

## 4. Chapter 1 — The Tale of Bart Sheen (canon per Leon, 29 Oct)
1. **Opening** — mid-toast, Bart's leg cramps, he falls off Tancrede into a hay bale. First chore: Work the Knot (tutorial).
2. **Quests** — Tourney at Amboise; Road Escort (Bart naps, squire quietly handles the danger; the Baron "happens" to meet them); optional Feast Challenge (all night, duel at dawn).
3. **The Pass** — a REAL cramp hits mid-pass. Bart goes down. Comedy stops dead; tone turns sincere.
4. **The Doctor's Visit (Leon's scene, locked):**
   - Doctor: "You may never walk again."
   - Bart: "Is that ale?" — gets up and starts walking to it.
   - Doctor has him held down, orders to stay down.
   - Bart: "That's a close one." — and secretly gets the ale anyway.
5. **The Trick** — Bart is tricked by the Doctor and the Baron. They make the injury sound worse than it is. The Baron offers to pay for surgery in Paris and Bart's debts — if the squire is handed over. Bart fights it and loses.
6. **Last order** (exact wording, every script): "Go to my cousin, he'll look after you, my boy."
7. **Packing scene** — short top-down walk; the squire packs Bart's goblet. No text.
8. Bart goes to Paris for surgery. The squire takes the lance — "First Lance."
