## 1. Tone rules (locked)
- Comedy, tragedy, secrets. Never explain a joke. Never explain a twist.
- The squire stays cool (Sang-froid meter; Trend Book tracks who copies him).
- Bart-horse cramps are SHOWN, never named or explained.
- Setting: France 1515–1559. Titles Squire -> Archduke. Six chapters: Montresor, Tournament Circuit, Cloth of Gold, Pavia, Fontainebleau, 1559 Joust.

## 2. Cast
- **Lord Bart Sheen** — drunk, warm, wins by accident, takes the credit ("the sun was in my goblet"). Loves ale more than sense. Kind underneath; hides it. Royal: THIRD in line (after the King and the 5-year-old heir) and does not care. Believes he owes the Baron, but those debts were paid long ago. He just likes to party.
- **Charles de Wierd** — spelled "Wierd" on purpose. Keeps hitting on Bart; nobody ever acknowledges him. Accidentally saves the squire (section 6).
- **The Squire** — 16 in 1515. Cool, quiet, competent. Shows no emotion and talks ONLY to Bart. Real name never revealed. Bart named him.
- **Tancrede** — the horse from the burned inn. Has opinions. Hates Bart's singing.
- **Baron of Vaudrecourt** (working name; names may change) — Bart's cousin. Sober, polished, generous in public, cold in private. Pays only when he gets something back. ILLEGITIMATE, never legitimized — far down the line of succession. Because he is illegitimate, the family curse does not touch him: his greed is entirely his own.
- **The Baron's Doctor** — the Baron's man. Gloomy pronouncements, sticky fingers.
- **Bayard** — the squire's mentor from Chapter 2, after Bart is gone (director call).
- **Time travelers** (running gag): Cartoonist, Tourist, Influencer, Historian.

## 2a. Core & family rules (canon)
- CORE: the squire needs the knight; the knight needs the serious squire to keep him in check.
- CURSE: in this royal family, lusting for power is a curse. The Baron, being illegitimate, is exempt.
- "JUST THERE": family members usually get crowned because they were the only royal who was "just there." The Baron must fight for what the rest stumble into.

## 3. Backstory (shown only in fragments)
~6 years before 1515, Bart, lost and drunk on the Loire road, finds a ~10-year-old boy at a burned inn calmly holding a panicking horse (Tancrede). Bart takes both. He tells everyone he "won the boy at dice" — a lie to hide that he was kind.
Fragments: scorched inn sign in the stable nobody may take down; Loire-road side quest where the squire stops at the ruined gate and the scene holds, no text.

## 4. Chapter 1 — The Tale of Bart Sheen (canon per Leon, 29 Oct)
1. **Opening** — mid-toast, Bart's leg cramps, he falls off Tancrede into a hay bale. First chore: Work the Knot (tutorial).
2. **Quests** — Tourney at Amboise; Road Escort (Bart naps, squire quietly handles the danger; the Baron "happens" to meet them); optional Feast Challenge (all night, duel at dawn).
3. **The Pass** — a REAL cramp hits mid-pass. Bart goes down. Comedy stops dead; tone turns sincere.
4. **The Doctor's Visit (Leon's scene, locked):**
   - Doctor: "You may never walk again." (Diagnosis: "a Charles Horse." Charles de Wierd thinks every mention is about him. Nobody corrects him.)
   - Bart: "Is that ale?" — gets up and starts walking to it.
   - Doctor has him held down, orders to stay down.
   - Bart: "That's a close one." — and secretly gets the ale anyway.
5. **The Trick** — Bart is tricked by the Doctor and the Baron. They make the injury sound worse than it is. The Baron offers to pay for surgery in Paris and Bart's debts — if the squire is handed over. Bart fights it and loses.
6. **Last order** (exact wording, every script): "Go to my cousin, he'll look after you, my boy."
7. **Packing scene** — short top-down walk; the squire packs Bart's goblet. No text.
8. Bart goes to Paris for surgery. The squire takes the lance — "First Lance."

## 5. Bart in Paris (canon)
- Bart slips out of the Baron's and the Doctor's grasp by **following a butterfly**. Never explained.
- He goes to **Château de Sampsonne** (renamed from "Castle de Simpson" to avoid copyright, GEN call).
- Player never SEES Paris directly; news reaches the squire in fragments (sober letter, inn rumour, surgeon's bill in the ledger).

## 6. The Squire's Journey (canon)
- Under the Baron: ringer jousts, thrown-match wagers, shady errands. Ledger: "Surgeons, Paris: paid. Squire: received." "LL" stamp gag.
- Loyal to Bart, not resentful. Keeps the goblet. Sets out on a mission to reach Paris.
- He almost makes it — but the Baron sends a paid knight to convince him Bart is dead.
- About to fall on his sword, Charles de Wierd walks in and accidentally spills the truth (unacknowledged, as always).
- The squire pursues Bart to Château de Sampsonne.

## 7. Château de Sampsonne (canon)
- Bart learns he has become King (he was "just there")... and doesn't care.
- The Baron gathers champions and pushes his weak claim to the throne. EPIC BATTLE.

## 8. Endings (GEN call: Ending 3 is main; 1 and 2 are alternates)
**Ending 3 — The Final Joust (MAIN).** The Baron's last champion is Bart, healed and sober, still believing his debts are owed. Player chooses:
- Win: Bart is unhorsed and laughs, finally free.
- Lose: Bart sees how hard the boy fought and switches sides.
- Yield: for the first time the squire speaks to someone other than Bart — reads the ledger aloud, "Squire: received." The Baron is exposed.
All paths reunite knight and squire.
**Alt 1 — The Crown Nobody Wants.** Squire beats the last champion while Bart feasts; the Baron's claim collapses; Bart hands the throne to the 5-year-old heir, names the squire protector, goes back to partying. Last shot: squire pours Bart's goblet — water — Bart drinks it anyway.
**Alt 2 — The Curse Turns.** The Baron wins and is crowned; the herald reads the old charter: whoever seizes the crown by force takes the family name, and the curse with it. Paranoia ruins him. Bart and the squire ride off; Charles de Wierd waves; nobody waves back.
