# Joustline: Mechanics (mobile, portrait, one thumb)

## Joust (about 40 seconds, 3 passes)
1. **Gallop:** hold to build speed. Hold too long and the horse stumbles.
2. **Aim:** drag a swaying reticle onto the shield, helm or chest. It sways more as you go faster.
3. **Strike:** release inside the gold window for Perfect, Good or Miss.
**Scoring:** shield or body = 1 point, helm = 2, unhorsing = instant win. Perfect hits get slow motion and a haptic buzz, and guarding is automatic.

## Other modes
Quintain (the daily challenge), ring tilt, swipe melee (Ch.3 onward) and court dialogue cards with Bold, Courtly or Wit replies.

## Progression
- **Favor:** -100 to +100 with each character.
- **Renown:** unlocks tournaments.
- **Gear:** Power, Precision, Guard and Stamina.
- **Horses:** train and breed them.
- **Montrésor estate:** earns coin while you're away.

## Mobile rules
Portrait 9:16 with controls in the thumb zone. Works at 360px wide. 3–5 minute sessions with autosave. Plays offline and syncs to LeonLink. 30fps battery mode. Installable as a PWA. Built with HTML5 canvas and vanilla JS.

## Money
Ch.1 is free. Later chapters unlock with LeonLink tokens. Cosmetics only, no pay-to-win and no energy timers.
