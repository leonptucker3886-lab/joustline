# Joustline: Plot
6 chapters, each with 8–12 phone sessions of 3–5 minutes.

1. **Montrésor (1515), free:** Bayard trains you. Win the fair joust.
2. **Tournament circuit (1516–19):** Leonardo designs your armor and Francis I knights you. Choose Queen Claude's household or Bonnivet's riders.
3. **Field of the Cloth of Gold (1520):** beat Charles Brandon and become Seigneur.
4. **Pavia (1524–26):** Bayard dies and the king is captured. Raise his ransom. Stay loyal or follow Bourbon.
5. **Fontainebleau (1530s–40s):** marriages, estates and court factions on the way to Duc.
6. **Last tournament (1559):** Witness Henri II's death and end as Duc. Or take the hidden path: max favor with Catherine plus the Leonardo helm lets you save him and become Archduke. The Bourbon path ends as Imperial Archduke, exiled from France.

**Titles:** Squire → Knight → Seigneur → Baron → Vicomte → Comte → Marquis → Duc → Archduke
