# Joustline: Plot
Canon source: docs/STORY_BIBLE.md (v0.4, locked). Aligned 30 Oct per GEN Paperwork: the main ending is the Final Joust against Lord Bart Sheen at Château de Sampsonne.
6 chapters, each with 8–12 phone sessions of 3–5 minutes.

## Chapter map
1. **Montrésor (1515), free — The Tale of Bart Sheen.** Opening (mid-toast cramp, hay bale, Work the Knot); quests (Tourney at Amboise, Road Escort, optional Feast Challenge); the real cramp mid-pass; the Doctor's Visit ("a Charles Horse"); the Trick (Doctor and Baron, surgery in Paris for the squire); last order: "Go to my cousin, he'll look after you, my boy."; packing scene; Bart to Paris; **First Lance**.
2. **Tournament circuit (1516–19) — The Baron's Household.** Bayard becomes the squire's mentor. Ringer jousts, thrown-match wagers, shady errands, the Debt ledger (the Baron's lie). Leonardo designs the helm. Fragments of news about Bart in Paris (sober letter, inn rumour, surgeon's bill). Bart escapes by following a butterfly (off screen).
3. **Field of the Cloth of Gold (1520) — The Road to Paris.** Beat Charles Brandon. The squire sets out to reach Bart.
4. **Pavia (1524–26) — The Fake Death.** Bayard dies; the king is captured. The Baron's paid knight convinces the squire Bart is dead. About to fall on his sword, Charles de Wierd walks in and accidentally spills the truth.
5. **Fontainebleau (1530s–40s) — Pursuit to Sampsonne.** Court factions; Catherine's favor; the squire tracks Bart to Château de Sampsonne, where Bart has become King ("just there") and keeps trying to give the crown to the Baron, who never hears.
6. **1559 — Sampsonne.** Saving Henri II, Leonardo's helm and Catherine's favor feed into the battle. The Baron gathers champions and pushes his weak claim. Epic battle, then **the Final Joust**.

## Endings
**Main — Ending 3, The Final Joust.** The Baron's last champion is Bart, healed and sober, still believing his debts are owed. Win (Bart unhorsed, laughs, free), Lose (Bart switches sides), or Yield (the squire speaks to someone other than Bart for the first time and reads the ledger: "Squire: received."; the Baron is exposed). All paths reunite knight and squire.
**Alternates:** Alt 1 The Crown Nobody Wants; Alt 2 The Curse Turns. **Unlockable:** Alt 3 The Ledger Joust; Alt A Just There; Alt B The Crown Nobody Hears. **Joke endings** (after main): The Tourist, The Inspector, The Shorts. Full text in STORY_BIBLE.md §8–8c.

There is no Bourbon-traitor path and no Archduke ending.

**Titles:** Squire → Knight → Seigneur → Baron → Vicomte → Comte → Marquis → Duc → Archduke (rank progression only).
