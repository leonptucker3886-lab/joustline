# The Final Joust: Bart Returns
Canon: Story Bible v0.4 §8 (Ending 3, MAIN). Aligned 30 Oct per GEN Paperwork. Original concept: Leon, 2026-10-15.

- **Where:** Château de Sampsonne, after the epic battle. This is the last joust of the game.
- **Who:** the Baron's last champion is **Lord Bart Sheen**, healed and sober, still believing his debts to the Baron are owed (they were paid long ago).
- **Bart returns.** The player last saw him carted to Paris; since then only fragments (sober letter, inn rumour, surgeon's bill) and Charles de Wierd's accidental news that he is alive.
- **Hardest joust in the game.** "Wins by accident" was the drunk Bart. Healed, sober and fighting for a debt he believes is real, he rides at his true best: luck is no longer needed.
- **Codependence, reversed:** the boy who kept Bart in the saddle now has to knock him out of it.
- Comedy survives the reunion (a cheer from across the lists, a goblet raised before the charge), then goes quiet for the passes.
- **Secrets stay shown, not told:** the Baron's ledger, the "LL" stamp, Bart refusing to look at the stands.
- Road to the lists: saving Henri II, Leonardo's helm and Catherine's favor feed into the battle.

## Outcomes (locked)
- **Win:** Bart is unhorsed and laughs, finally free.
- **Lose:** Bart sees how hard the boy fought and switches sides.
- **Yield:** for the first time the squire speaks to someone other than Bart. He reads the ledger aloud: "Squire: received." The Baron is exposed.
All paths reunite knight and squire.

## Achievements (proposed, for ACHIEVEMENTS.md)
- **Unhorsed, Unbound** (+60 XP): win the Final Joust.
- **Switching Sides** (+60 XP): lose the Final Joust.
- **Squire: Received** (+80 XP): yield and read the ledger.
