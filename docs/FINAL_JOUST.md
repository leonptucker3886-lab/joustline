# The Final Joust: Bart Returns (Leon, 2026-10-15)

- **To become Archduke, the squire must face Bart.** This is the last joust of the game.
- **Bart comes back here.** After the Paris surgery his fate is never confirmed (rumours only). This is the first time the player sees him again.
- He rides in someone else's colours, almost certainly arranged by the Baron, whose ledger has always owned Bart's debt. The game never says this outright.
- **Best jouster in France, healed leg.** The cramp that ruined him is gone, so this is Bart at his true best: the hardest joust in the game.
- **Codependence, reversed:** the boy who kept Bart in the saddle now has to knock him out of it.
- Comedy survives the reunion (a "WINNING!" from across the lists, a goblet raised before the charge), then goes quiet for the passes.
- **The game never tells its secrets:** what Bart knows about the trade, and why he is here, is shown only through small details (the Baron's ledger, the "LL" stamp, Bart refusing to look at the stands).
- Open: win/lose/yield outcomes and achievements for this joust.
