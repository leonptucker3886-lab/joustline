# Joustline: Character List
Mobile game. Each character has a portrait card (illuminated-manuscript style) and a favor meter.

## You
Squire of Montrésor, age 14 in 1515. You pick your name, crest and one trait: Bold (+power), Courtly (+favor) or Shrewd (+coin).

## Royal family
- **Francis I** (1494–1547): the king, your patron and the one who grants titles.
- **Louise of Savoy**: queen mother and regent. Controls the money.
- **Marguerite de Navarre**: the king's sister and a writer. Opens the "Wit" path.
- **Queen Claude**: gives you your first court post.
- **Henri II** (1519–1559): first a rival, later a friend. Dies in the 1559 joust.
- **Catherine de' Medici**: a patient, dangerous ally.

## Knights
- **Bayard**: your mentor, "without fear and beyond reproach". Dies in 1524.
- **Montmorency**: a stern sponsor who rewards loyalty.
- **Duke of Bourbon**: defects to the Emperor and offers you a betrayal path.
- **Bonnivet**: your rival for honors. Dies at Pavia.
- **Montgomery**: the Scots Guard captain who fatally wounds Henri II.

## Foreign
**Henry VIII**, **Charles Brandon** (Ch.3 boss) and **Charles V**, who can grant the Archduke title.

## Arts
**Leonardo da Vinci** (designs your armor, dies 1519), **Diane de Poitiers**, the poet **Clément Marot**, **Cellini** and **Rosso Fiorentino**.

## Fictional
**Old Gaspard** (stablemaster and tutorial voice), **Isabeau de Loches** (rival squire who becomes a friend), **Brother Anselme** (runs the estate) and **Tancrède**, your first horse.
