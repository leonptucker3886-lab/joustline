# Joustline: Character List
Mobile game. Each character has a portrait card (illuminated-manuscript style) and a favor meter.
Canon source: docs/STORY_BIBLE.md (v0.4, locked). Aligned 30 Oct per GEN Paperwork: Bible v0.4 is canon; the knight is Lord Bart Sheen.

## You: the Squire
Squire of Montrésor, age **16** in 1515. Cool, quiet, competent. Shows no emotion and talks **only to Bart**. His real name is never revealed: Bart named him. The player picks his crest and one trait: Bold (+power), Courtly (+favor) or Shrewd (+coin). No name pick.
Backstory (fragments only): ~6 years before 1515 Bart found him, about 10, at a burned inn on the Loire road, calmly holding a panicking horse (Tancrède). Bart says he "won the boy at dice", a lie to hide that he was kind.

## Lord Bart Sheen (canon)
- Drunk, warm, wins by accident, takes the credit ("the sun was in my goblet"). Kind underneath; hides it.
- Royal: **third in line** (after the King and the 5-year-old heir) and does not care.
- Believes he owes the Baron, but those debts were paid long ago. He just likes to party.
- His cramps are shown, never explained. Only the Doctor names it: "a Charles Horse."
- Fate: survives. Escapes in Paris by following a butterfly, becomes King at Château de Sampsonne ("just there"), and is the Baron's last champion in the Final Joust.
- Bloodline hints (optional texture, no named dynasty reveal in canon): a signet ring with a worn crest; an old servant who bows a little too low; Bart unknowingly getting court etiquette right.
- The name is a nod to a famous party-loving actor; no text ever spells out the real name.

## Bart's world (Bible §2–7)
- **Charles de Wierd**: spelled "Wierd" on purpose. Keeps hitting on Bart; nobody ever acknowledges him. Accidentally saves the squire by spilling the truth that Bart is alive.
- **Baron of Vaudrecourt** (working name): Bart's cousin. Sober, polished, generous in public, cold in private. Illegitimate, never legitimized, so the family curse does not touch him; his greed is his own. Takes the squire through the Trick, runs him as a ringer, sends the fake-death knight, and pushes a weak claim to the throne at Sampsonne.
- **The Baron's Doctor**: gloomy pronouncements, sticky fingers. Diagnoses "a Charles Horse" and helps the Baron make the injury sound worse.
- **The 5-year-old heir**: second in line. Central to Alt 1 and Alt A.
- **Tancrède**: the horse from the burned inn. Has opinions. Hates Bart's singing.
- **The paid knight**: sent by the Baron to convince the squire Bart is dead.

## Family rules (canon)
- CURSE: in this royal family, lusting for power is a curse. The Baron, being illegitimate, is exempt.
- "JUST THERE": family members get crowned because they were the only royal who was there.

## Historical figures (public history)
- **Francis I**, **Louise of Savoy**, **Marguerite de Navarre**, **Queen Claude**.
- **Henri II** (1519–1559): first a rival, later a friend. Saving him feeds into the Sampsonne battle.
- **Catherine de' Medici**: a patient, dangerous ally. Her favor feeds into the battle.
- **Bayard**: the squire's mentor **from Chapter 2**, after Bart is gone. Dies in 1524.
- **Montmorency**, **Bonnivet** (dies at Pavia), **Montgomery** (Scots Guard captain).
- **Duke of Bourbon**: historical defector to the Emperor. **No betrayal path** for the player.
- Foreign: **Henry VIII**, **Charles Brandon** (Ch.3 boss), **Charles V**.
- Arts: **Leonardo da Vinci** (designs the helm that feeds into the battle; dies 1519), **Diane de Poitiers**, **Clément Marot**, **Cellini**, **Rosso Fiorentino**.
